The Best Method

Every great cut starts
with a read.

Head shape, crown, hairline, growth pattern. Most shops skip all of it. We start there, and that's why our clients come back.

  1. 01

    The Read

    We look at your head shape, profile and hairline, and talk through your lifestyle. Then we recommend what will actually suit you. Honest advice, no guesswork.

  2. 02

    The Cut

    A smooth blend, a crisp taper and a clean line, all done at a pace that puts precision first.

  3. 03

    The Finish

    Razor detail, hot towel, styled to walk out. We'll also tell you how to keep it looking sharp until your next visit.

Good to Know

Questions,
answered.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, whenever a chair is open. If you want a guaranteed time, especially on weekends, book ahead.

I don't know what cut I want. Can you help?

That's what we're known for. Daniel will look at your head shape, hairline and how you wear your hair day to day, then recommend a cut that suits you.

How often should I get my fade touched up?

Every 2–3 weeks keeps a skin or low fade crisp. A taper and lineup between full cuts is a quick, affordable way to stay sharp.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Absolutely. We're patient with first-timers and precise with the details.

What payment do you accept?

We accept cash and all major cards.
