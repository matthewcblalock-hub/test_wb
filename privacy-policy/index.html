Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

Creech Heating and Air respects your privacy. This policy explains what information this website collects and how it's used.

Information you give us

When you submit a service request, we collect the details you enter: your name, phone number, email address (if provided), service address or ZIP code, a description of your heating or cooling problem, and your contact preferences.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your request, schedule and provide service, and communicate with you about it. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Phone calls, texts, and email

If you ask us to contact you, we'll use the method you choose. If you choose text messaging, message and data rates may apply. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time.

Service providers

Form submissions are delivered through a third-party form-processing service, which handles the data only to pass your request along to us.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices

To ask what information we have about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, contact us using the details on our homepage.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
