HVAC Maintenance & Inspections

When to Call

Signs it's time to call

  • It's been a while since your system was last looked at
  • You want to get ahead of the heating or cooling season
  • The system works, but it doesn't perform like it used to
  • You've moved into a home and don't know the equipment's history
  • You've noticed a new sound, smell, or change in airflow
  • You'd like an honest read on how much life the system has left

Our Approach

How Creech handles it

  • Honest findings

    If something needs attention, you'll hear what it is and how urgent it really is. If it doesn't, you'll hear that too.

  • Practical next steps

    Small issues are explained clearly, so you can plan ahead instead of getting surprised in the middle of a cold snap.

  • A relationship, not a one-off

    Many customers have relied on Creech Heating and Air for years. Ask about what's included in a maintenance visit for your system.
